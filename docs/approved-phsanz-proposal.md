## Purpose and governance

PH Navigator is a patient-centred digital navigation platform addressing prolonged PH/PAH diagnostic journeys, self-management burdens, and gaps in lived-experience resources and system navigation. The proposal envisages an accredited platform initially within the PHSANZ digital domain, governed by its Patient Advocacy Subcommittee.

Chris and Tharuka (lived-experience representatives) are the planned Patient Advocacy Co-Leads, guiding content strategy, community engagement and multi-agency alignment. A designated PHSANZ clinical sponsor is to assure medical accuracy and alignment with registry priorities and clinical statements.

## Stage 3 — conditional matched funding

Target approximately AUD $18,000–$22,000 (about $20,000), using the PHSANZ seed commitment as leverage; this is a funding target, not secured funding.

- Lung Foundation Australia (LFA): proposed formal MoU for medically vetted, co-branded printable flyers and digital toolkits, modelled on the CRE-PF resource suite.
- Pulmonary Hypertension Association Australia (PHAA): peer recruitment, community review panels and two-way referral integration.
- Pharmaceutical/independent medical education grants: potential partners named include MSD, Janssen, Boehringer Ingelheim and AstraZeneca; no grant commitments are established by the proposal.
