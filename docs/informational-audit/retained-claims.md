# Supported and qualified material to retain

These checks prevent the audit from treating every simplification as an error.
They are scoped to the stated claim, not blanket clinical approval of a page.
Source IDs resolve to [the source register](sources.md), which declares access limits.

| Existing material | Assessment and limit | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PH concerns pressure in the pulmonary circulation, distinct from an arm-cuff blood-pressure reading | Retain the basic explanation; qualify umbrella PH versus rare PAH and cause-dependent mechanisms | UK04, UK05, CL01; INF-10 |
| Five clinical cause groups | Retain the organising framework; revise specific sleep-apnoea/genetics examples and distinguish functional class | UK05, CL01, CL02; INF-11/12, GAP-06 |
| mPAP >20 mmHg at rest on RHC | Boundary is supported; do not label this as the obsolete ≥25 definition or infer treatment eligibility | CL01; INF-01 |
| PAWP ≤15 versus >15 mmHg | Boundary is supported; two groups cannot be distinguished on this number alone | CL01; INF-02 |
| RHC measures haemodynamics directly; echo estimates and assesses cardiac effects | Retain the purpose; avoid automatic testing instructions for every suggestive echo | UK06, CL01/02; INF-13 |
| ERA receptor-blocking concept | Retain receptor-antagonist framing; do not replace it with a different simplified mechanism taken from a benchmark page | AU28, UK33; full current PI review still required for drug-specific detail |
| Riociguat can have a role in CTEPH | Retain with correct selected-patient scope, interaction warnings and separate local access pathway | AU02, AU09; INF-04/05 |
| Potentially curative PEA in selected CTEPH patients | Retain the caveat; reconcile blanket no-cure language and avoid a BPA cure guarantee | AU02, CL02, UK10; INF-09, detailed specialist review remains required |
| Salt and sodium are different quantities | Existing 2,000 mg sodium to approximately 5 g salt conversion is supported; universal PH prescription is not established | AU12; INF-15 |
| Warfarin and consistency of vitamin-K-containing foods | Retain consistency/monitoring advice rather than telling patients to eliminate all green vegetables; no universal INR/dose target | AU27 |
| Activity should be discussed with the PH team | Retain specialist discussion and pacing; qualify stability, supervision, symptoms and activity examples | UK16, CL02; INF-16/18, full exercise review still required |
| Existing Medicare ten-individual-session concept | Supported; add eligibility/referral, separate group-session allowance and possible gap fees | AU10; INF-30 |
| NDIS access is not automatic from a PH diagnosis | Retain this correct caveat; remove unsupported frequency/guarantee language and selective evidence advice | AU05, AU06; INF-22 |
| Carer Gateway routine weekday hours | Supported for routine enquiries; emergency respite can be accessed differently and depends on suitable local services | AU14; INF-26 |
| Appointment question page | Useful patient-preparation format; not an unavailable PDF and not an assertion that every question applies to every patient | Internal inventory, UK27/28; GAP-24/25 |
| Community, resource and participation development labels | Accurate for the inspected repository state; do not count these as fabricated services or activate them to match PHA UK | Internal inventory and user-approved project direction |

Further verification is needed for details such as individual procedure
preparation, medicine monitoring schedules, weight/fluid triggers, local oxygen
funding, state-specific legal forms and service availability. They are not marked
verified merely because a broad concept is familiar or appears on another site.
