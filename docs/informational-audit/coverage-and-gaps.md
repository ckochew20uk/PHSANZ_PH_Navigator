## Development briefs

### GAP-01 — Symptoms, deterioration and a personal action plan

Explain emergency vs same-day team contact vs routine questions; country-labelled contacts, personal team/after-hours plan and infusion exception.

**Proposed reviewer:** Clinician Advisory Panel and PH nurse. **Gate:** Draft, review and publish within the existing navigation; no new collection service.

**Evidence/benchmark:** [UK04](sources.md#uk04), [UK09](sources.md#uk09), [AU21](sources.md#au21), [AU22](sources.md#au22), [NZ01](sources.md#nz01).

### GAP-02 — Pregnancy, contraception and reproductive decisions

Sensitive, current risk communication, preconception advice, unplanned-pregnancy contact, medicine-specific precautions, contraception interactions and specialist counselling. No unsupported blanket mortality percentage.

**Proposed reviewer:** PH clinician, reproductive-health specialist and lived-experience reviewers. **Gate:** Draft, review and publish within the existing navigation; no new collection service.

**Evidence/benchmark:** [UK20](sources.md#uk20), [AU01](sources.md#au01), [AU02](sources.md#au02), [CL04](sources.md#cl04).

### GAP-03 — Medicine safety, monitoring, supply and interruption

Current CMI links, interaction check, monitoring plan, side-effect triage, safe missed-dose signposting, travel/supply continuity and exact continuous-infusion plan.

**Proposed reviewer:** PH pharmacist and infusion-trained nurse with clinician sign-off. **Gate:** Draft, review and publish within the existing navigation; no new collection service.

**Evidence/benchmark:** [UK09](sources.md#uk09), [UK30](sources.md#uk30), [AU01](sources.md#au01), [AU02](sources.md#au02), [AU08](sources.md#au08), [AU09](sources.md#au09).

### GAP-04 — CTEPH as a distinct diagnosis and treatment journey

Explain investigation, multidisciplinary operability assessment, PEA/BPA/medicine roles, follow-up and anticoagulation without universal instructions or guaranteed cure.

**Proposed reviewer:** CTEPH specialist team. **Gate:** Draft, review and publish within the existing navigation; no new collection service.

**Evidence/benchmark:** [UK05](sources.md#uk05), [UK10](sources.md#uk10), [AU02](sources.md#au02), [AU09](sources.md#au09), [CL02](sources.md#cl02).

### GAP-05 — Other PH groups and treatment boundaries

Clearly distinguish Group 1 PAH from left-heart/lung-disease PH and other causes; show why another person’s medicine may not be appropriate.

**Proposed reviewer:** Clinician Advisory Panel. **Gate:** Draft, review and publish within the existing navigation; no new collection service.

**Evidence/benchmark:** [UK05](sources.md#uk05), [CL01](sources.md#cl01), [CL02](sources.md#cl02).

### GAP-06 — WHO functional class, groups, risk and test trends

Separate cause-group from functional class; explain that walk tests, biomarkers, imaging and trends are interpreted together, not as self-scored prognosis. Do not copy EmPHasis-10.

**Proposed reviewer:** Clinician Advisory Panel. **Gate:** Draft, review and publish within the existing navigation; no new collection service.

**Evidence/benchmark:** [UK05](sources.md#uk05), [UK25](sources.md#uk25), [UK29](sources.md#uk29), [CL01](sources.md#cl01), [CL03](sources.md#cl03).

### GAP-07 — What to expect from investigations

Explain RHC, echo, V/Q, CT and lung-function tests in plain language; local instructions come from the treating service, not generic fasting/medicine-hold directions.

**Proposed reviewer:** Clinician Advisory Panel and relevant diagnostic nurses. **Gate:** Draft, review and publish within the existing navigation; no new collection service.

**Evidence/benchmark:** [UK06](sources.md#uk06), [CL01](sources.md#cl01), [CL02](sources.md#cl02).

### GAP-08 — Home oxygen and equipment safety

Purpose, prescribed use, fire/smoking safety, supplier/backup arrangements and equipment planning; verify state funding and avoid generic flow rates.

**Proposed reviewer:** Clinician/oxygen-service reviewer; Australian supplier/funding facts require further verification. **Gate:** Draft, review and publish within the existing navigation; no new collection service.

**Evidence/benchmark:** [UK14](sources.md#uk14), [AU11](sources.md#au11).

### GAP-09 — Fatigue, pacing, sleep and everyday routines

Develop realistic daily-life examples, rest/activity balance, sleep concerns and support without implying fatigue is a motivation problem.

**Proposed reviewer:** Occupational therapist, PH nurse and Lived Experience Panel. **Gate:** Draft, review and publish within the existing navigation; no new collection service.

**Evidence/benchmark:** [UK12](sources.md#uk12), [UK16](sources.md#uk16), [AU21](sources.md#au21).

### GAP-10 — Sexual health, intimacy and relationships

Patient-centred discussion prompts, adapting intimacy, communication and medicine concerns; no copied booklet or universal exercise equivalence.

**Proposed reviewer:** PH clinician, sexual-health clinician and Lived Experience Panel. **Gate:** Draft, review and publish within the existing navigation; no new collection service.

**Evidence/benchmark:** [UK22](sources.md#uk22), [UK15](sources.md#uk15), [AU01](sources.md#au01), [AU02](sources.md#au02).

### GAP-11 — Menopause and changing life stages

Assess patient needs and provide current specialist-reviewed symptom/treatment signposting; do not claim a UK hormone recommendation applies in Australia.

**Proposed reviewer:** Relevant specialist and Lived Experience Panel; additional evidence required. **Gate:** Draft, review and publish within the existing navigation; no new collection service.

**Evidence/benchmark:** [UK03](sources.md#uk03), [AU01](sources.md#au01), [AU02](sources.md#au02).

### GAP-12 — Driving, parking permits and mobility

Use Australian fitness-to-drive assessment and state/territory licensing/permit sources; no blanket DVLA rule or universal right to a permit.

**Proposed reviewer:** Clinician and Australian licensing/transport reviewer. **Gate:** Draft, review and publish within the existing navigation; no new collection service.

**Evidence/benchmark:** [UK13](sources.md#uk13), [AU20](sources.md#au20).

### GAP-13 — Employment, study and reasonable adjustments

Disclosing a condition, adjustments, flexible work/study, leave, social-work and legal signposting; verify Fair Work, education and insurer-specific claims separately.

**Proposed reviewer:** Social worker/employment-rights reviewer; additional primary legal sources needed. **Gate:** Draft, review and publish within the existing navigation; no new collection service.

**Evidence/benchmark:** [UK12](sources.md#uk12), [UK19](sources.md#uk19), [AU07](sources.md#au07), [AU16](sources.md#au16).

### GAP-14 — Paediatric PH and transition to adult care

State adult scope until reviewed; develop age-appropriate family/young-person and transition guidance rather than applying adult tests/treatments automatically.

**Proposed reviewer:** Paediatric PH specialist and young-person/family reviewers. **Gate:** Draft, review and publish within the existing navigation; no new collection service.

**Evidence/benchmark:** [UK21](sources.md#uk21), [AU04](sources.md#au04), [CL01](sources.md#cl01).

### GAP-15 — Talking with children about a parent’s PH

Age-appropriate conversations and support routes; distinguish this topic from a child personally living with PH.

**Proposed reviewer:** Psychology/child-family practitioner and lived-experience reviewers. **Gate:** Draft, review and publish within the existing navigation; no new collection service.

**Evidence/benchmark:** [UK21](sources.md#uk21), [AU10](sources.md#au10).

### GAP-16 — Heritable PAH, genetics and family screening

Explain the role and limits of genetic counselling/testing and family assessment without inferring deterministic inheritance or giving an unverified risk percentage.

**Proposed reviewer:** PH/genetics specialists. **Gate:** Draft, review and publish within the existing navigation; no new collection service.

**Evidence/benchmark:** [UK05](sources.md#uk05), [CL01](sources.md#cl01), [CL02](sources.md#cl02).

### GAP-17 — Supportive/palliative care and planning ahead

Explain symptom support, personal goals and planning as compatible with ongoing treatment; separately localise legal documents and service access.

**Proposed reviewer:** PH/palliative-care clinicians and Lived Experience Panel. **Gate:** Draft, review and publish within the existing navigation; no new collection service.

**Evidence/benchmark:** [UK31](sources.md#uk31), [AU17](sources.md#au17).

### GAP-18 — Vaccination, infection prevention and intercurrent illness

Prompt individual vaccination review and an illness/contact plan. Verify the current Australian Immunisation Handbook and funding separately; no hard-coded universal vaccine schedule.

**Proposed reviewer:** Clinician/immunisation reviewer. **Gate:** Draft, review and publish within the existing navigation; no new collection service.

**Evidence/benchmark:** [UK12](sources.md#uk12), [AU23](sources.md#au23).

### GAP-19 — Rural/remote access, telehealth and local-team coordination

Explain local GP/PH team coordination, telehealth limits, travel planning, prescription continuity and state-specific assistance; do not imply everyone must move or travel to a capital.

**Proposed reviewer:** Rural-care/social-work reviewer and relevant lived-experience contributors. **Gate:** Draft, review and publish within the existing navigation; no new collection service.

**Evidence/benchmark:** [UK14](sources.md#uk14), [AU04](sources.md#au04), [AU18](sources.md#au18), [AU19](sources.md#au19).

### GAP-20 — Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander cultural safety

Seek appropriate cultural review and co-design; include eligible aged-care/travel pathways without treating diverse communities as one or inventing Indigenous patient experiences.

**Proposed reviewer:** Appropriate Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander reviewers with clinical/service owners. **Gate:** Requires genuine participation, consent and suitable institutional arrangements; no tokenistic invented content.

**Evidence/benchmark:** [AU13](sources.md#au13), [AU18](sources.md#au18).

### GAP-21 — Languages, health literacy and accessible formats

Test comprehension, explain acronyms, provide text/print alternatives and qualified translation review. Do not present automatic medical translation as validated.

**Proposed reviewer:** Health-literacy/accessibility reviewers and qualified language reviewers. **Gate:** Draft, review and publish within the existing navigation; no new collection service.

**Evidence/benchmark:** [UK01](sources.md#uk01), [UK28](sources.md#uk28), [AU06](sources.md#au06).

### GAP-22 — Research, registries and clinical trials

Distinguish care, registry participation, clinical trials and website co-design; explain informed choice and verified trial signposting without recruitment promises.

**Proposed reviewer:** Clinician/research-governance reviewer; verify current trial/registry pages separately. **Gate:** Draft, review and publish within the existing navigation; no new collection service.

**Evidence/benchmark:** [UK01](sources.md#uk01), [UK07](sources.md#uk07), [AU04](sources.md#au04).

### GAP-23 — Real community voices

Develop a consent-led range of real experiences, including ordinary adaptations and uncertainty; avoid implying everyone achieves the same outcome.

**Proposed reviewer:** Lived Experience Panel and editorial/privacy owners. **Gate:** Real contributions, consent, editorial approval, attribution/withdrawal process; submissions remain closed.

**Evidence/benchmark:** [UK01](sources.md#uk01), [UK15](sources.md#uk15).

### GAP-24 — Useful printable resources

Prioritise original appointment questions/personal contact plan, diagnosis overview, medicines checklist and travel planner. Verify files and versions before enabling download actions.

**Proposed reviewer:** Clinical/editorial owners with lived-experience review. **Gate:** Approved real files, copyright permission, accessibility checks and version records. No mass reproduction of UK publications.

**Evidence/benchmark:** [UK02](sources.md#uk02), [UK27](sources.md#uk27), [UK28](sources.md#uk28), [AU11](sources.md#au11).

### GAP-25 — Newly diagnosed reassurance with practical next steps

Provide a short take-home guide: know your PH type/team, questions, medicines plan, symptoms/contact plan and support. Balance hope with individual variation; no unsupported survival figures.

**Proposed reviewer:** Clinician and Lived Experience Panels. **Gate:** Draft, review and publish within the existing navigation; no new collection service.

**Evidence/benchmark:** [UK03](sources.md#uk03), [UK04](sources.md#uk04), [AU21](sources.md#au21), [CL01](sources.md#cl01).

### GAP-26 — New Zealand scope boundary

Label Australian eligibility and contacts explicitly; maintain safe NZ emergency signposting and plan a separately evidenced NZ localisation, not Australian scheme substitution.

**Proposed reviewer:** Australian and New Zealand jurisdiction reviewers. **Gate:** This audit is Australian-focused and is not full NZ clinical/access validation.

**Evidence/benchmark:** [NZ01](sources.md#nz01), [AU05](sources.md#au05), [AU07](sources.md#au07), [AU10](sources.md#au10).
