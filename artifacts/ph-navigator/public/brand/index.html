PH NAVIGATOR · PROPOSED DIGITAL IDENTITY

One mark. A lighter voice.

A proposed logo and colour kit built from the supplied sailboat and heart artwork. Full-colour marks retain the original gradients. Wordmarks use outlined DM Sans Medium lettering so exported SVGs render without font downloads.

View the website · Download the complete digital kit

Use with care

Use the colour logo on white or off-white and the white logo on navy. Keep clear space around the artwork. Use the simplified icon for browser tabs, not a miniature wordmark. This is a suggested identity for review, not a claim of organisational approval.

Navy anchors headings and the footer. Deep teal supports accessible buttons and links. Bright sail teal and ocean blue are accents, not small text on white. Sea mist, lavender and peach provide occasional supporting surfaces; white and off-white remain dominant.
