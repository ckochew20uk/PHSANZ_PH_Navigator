# PH Navigator — proposed digital brand kit

Prepared 3 November 2026 from the user-supplied `Logo.svg`. The unmodified source is preserved in `design/brand/logo-master.svg` in the repository and `source/logo-master.svg` in the ZIP. The supplied artwork's original gradients are retained in colour exports. This is a proposed identity for review.

## Included versions

- Horizontal logo with “PH Navigator”: colour, white and navy; SVG and transparent 2160 × 520 PNG.
- Stacked logo with “PH Navigator”: colour, white and navy; SVG and transparent 1520 × 1440 PNG.
- Symbol only: colour, white and navy; SVG and transparent 1024 × 1024 PNG.
- Simplified browser icon: SVG, 16/32px PNG and multi-size ICO.
- Home-screen icon: opaque 180 × 180 PNG.
- Social card: 1200 × 630 PNG and editable SVG. Configure an absolute image URL in social metadata after selecting the public deployment URL; the kit does not assume a canonical host.
- `colours.json`, this guide and `index.html` visual preview.

SVG wordmark letters are outlined DejaVu Sans Bold. They do not require fonts on the viewing device. The original source remains editable; lettering is separately editable as paths in derived SVGs. Font attribution/license is included in FONT-LICENSE.txt. No fonts are embedded or downloaded by the logo assets.

## Usage

Use the horizontal colour logo in light headers and the white version on navy. Use stacked variants when a square or portrait space suits better. The mark is suitable for avatars, and the simplified icon for very small browser tabs. Do not use the complete horizontal logo at favicon size.

Allow clear space of at least one tenth of the symbol height on each side. Suggested minimum widths: horizontal 160px, stacked 150px, full symbol 48px. Below that, use the simplified icon and test at actual size. Keep proportions, do not stretch, add shadows or place colour artwork over busy photography. Transparent white PNGs must be viewed on a dark background.

## Palette

- Navy #172E5B: primary text, headings, footer and mono artwork.
- Deep teal #17748C: links, buttons and focus accents on light surfaces.
- Sail teal #159BA6 and ocean blue #2786BD: visual accents; do not assume sufficient contrast for small text.
- Sea mist #E3F2F2, lavender #ECEAF6 and peach #F8E2D5: occasional background surfaces.
- Off-white #F7F9FB and white #FFFFFF: dominant page backgrounds.

Shared website semantic HSL tokens closely approximate the source HEX colours. Logo SVGs and kit swatches retain the exact HEX values. Colour never replaces text labels or focus indication.

The uploaded mark contains no text. All wordmark layouts, monochrome versions, simplified icons and colour applications in this kit are proposals derived for this review; no organisational endorsement is implied.
